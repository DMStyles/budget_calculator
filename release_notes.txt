## What's New

### Dashboard & Monthly Focus
- The Home screen now focuses entirely on your **current month's budget**! Balance, Income, Expenses, and Category budgets will now intelligently filter out past data to give you a clear view of where you stand this month.
- To keep the dashboard clean, the Recent Transactions list is now capped at the 10 most recent entries.

### Data Management
- We've added a brand new **Data Management** section in the Settings screen!
- **Export Data**: Bundle all your transactions into a standard CSV file to save or share.
- **Import Data**: Pick a previously exported CSV file from your phone to easily restore your data back into the app!
