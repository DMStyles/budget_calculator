## What's New in v1.4.0

### Cloud Synchronization & Google Sign-In ☁️
- **Never lose your data again!** We have fully migrated Glass Budget from local storage to the cloud.
- A beautiful new **Google Sign-In** screen has been added to securely authenticate you.
- Your existing local transactions will **automatically migrate** to your secure cloud database the very first time you sign in!
- **User Profile**: A new profile card at the top of the Settings screen displays your Google account details and allows you to securely sign out.

### Security
- Integrated Row Level Security (RLS) so your financial data is strictly tied to your Google account. Nobody else can access it!
